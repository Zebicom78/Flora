Biophilic Living

Bringing Healing Greenery Into Daily Living

Studies continuously demonstrate that proximity to living greenery and daylight reduces cortisol levels, boosts cognitive clarity, and fosters deeper emotional connection.

At Flora City, every balcony includes engineered planter boxes with automated drip irrigation, vertical green screens that filter airborne particulate matter, and deep shade overhangs designed to cut down cooling loads.
