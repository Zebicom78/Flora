1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Flora City through our website, schedule a private site visit, or submit an enquiry form, we collect personal contact information including your full name, email address, mobile number, and specific property preferences.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. All data gathered is utilized strictly to provide tailored residential advisory services, schedule estate walk-throughs, and communicate project milestones.

Flora City employs industry-standard encryption protocols and secure server architectures. Access to your personal data is restricted solely to authorized relationship managers and concierge personnel obligated under strict confidentiality covenants.
