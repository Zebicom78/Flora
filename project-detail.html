Architectural Vision

A Living Botanical Sanctuary in Gwalior

Flora Botanica Residences is conceived as a serene enclave where architectural modernism yields gracefully to living nature. Spread over 9.5 lush acres with only 4 boutique towers, each apartment is oriented to maximize morning light and sweeping views of the central park.

With a zero-vehicle ground surface, children and seniors wander freely across shaded orchards, reflection ponds, and sensory herb gardens.
